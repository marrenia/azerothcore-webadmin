Realms

The realm list handed to clients after they authenticate.

Address is the one field that silently breaks logins. It is what the client is told to connect to after it authenticates, so a wrong value gives no login error — players just hang at “Logging in to game server”. It must be an address the client can reach, not necessarily one this server can. The authserver re-reads this table roughly every 20 seconds (RealmsStateUpdateDelay), so changes apply without a restart.
